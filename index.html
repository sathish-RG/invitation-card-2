Chapter One

Our Story

A borrowed umbrella, a missed train, and seven wonderful years later — here’s how we got to forever.

October 2019 · London

How We Met

A sudden downpour outside Paddington Station, one umbrella, and two strangers both late for the same delayed train. By the time it finally arrived, we’d already planned a second coffee. James still claims he “let” Eleanor keep the umbrella.

2020 – 2024 · Everywhere

The Journey

Long-distance calls across eight time zones, a move to San Francisco, a sourdough starter named Gerald, and a golden retriever named Biscuit. We learned that home isn’t a place — it’s each other.

September 2025 · Big Sur

The Proposal

On a cliffside hike at sunset — the same trail from our very first trip together — James dropped to one knee, Biscuit barked, and Eleanor said yes before he could finish the question.

“Whatever our souls are made of, his and mine are the same.”

Emily Brontë
